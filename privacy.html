Privacy Policy — Mr. Brad Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-15

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel and set their title, description and publish time;
  • read the list of videos already on that channel, so it does not upload the same video twice;
  • add uploaded videos to the channel's playlists.

How data is stored and shared

The sign-in token and the upload history are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs, nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and no data from other people is collected. The app has no users other than the channel owner.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.
